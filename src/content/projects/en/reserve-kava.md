challenge: The product page had to be rebuilt from a design handoff while the live store kept selling, and the client keeps refining the copy and tests changes. Each new product needed the same page without starting from scratch.
scope:
  - "Rebuild the product page to the design handoff: gallery, buy column and content sections."
  - Add a bundle that puts both jars in the cart, plus single and multi-jar options with subscription.
  - Prepare templates for further products that the client can set up from the theme editor.
solution:
  - Rebuilt the Borogu and Kelai pages to the handoff in a preview theme, with the client’s photos in the reasons cards and the comparison block.
  - Added the Relaxation Bundle, which adds both jars to the cart, and set up an unlisted test product to build the 3-jar and 5-jar selector against.
  - Created separate templates for two upcoming products so each opens on its own layout in the editor.
  - Fixed the gallery pinning, kept the header button on one line, ran the product marquee to the screen edge, and made home page product cards open the product page.
outcome:
  - The buy area is clearer and matches the client’s design.
  - New products arrive on a ready template instead of a new build.
  - Policy, shipping and info pages plus a full footer give the store a complete trust layer.
