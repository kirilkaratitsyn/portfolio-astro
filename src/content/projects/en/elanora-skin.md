challenge: The store sells in many markets and two languages and runs a steady stream of offers. It needed a technical partner who could ship promotions quickly, measure which version works, and keep prices, labels and gifts consistent in the cart and at checkout.
scope:
  - Support the Shopify storefront as an ongoing technical partner, with regular releases.
  - "Build offer logic that adapts by market and language: announcement bars, upsells, free gifts and free-shipping thresholds."
  - Wire A/B tests for new offers and report the results to the team.
  - Extend retention with a custom back-in-stock flow connected through the Klaviyo API.
solution:
  - Built a gift engine for the cart that adds free products as real lines with their own title, image and struck-through price, and sends them in a single cart update to keep the cart fast.
  - Set up offer pages and tiered offers with A/B wiring, so versions can run side by side and be switched on or off quickly.
  - Added checkout blocks, including a removable insurance extension and a reviews block, and kept discount labels correct in both languages.
  - Added back-in-stock logic through the Klaviyo API to support retention beyond standard theme work.
outcome:
  - Promotions ship faster and can be tested before they replace the control.
  - The cart shows free items clearly, with the right prices and labels in every market.
  - Ongoing support lets the store keep evolving without piling risky ad hoc fixes on the theme.
