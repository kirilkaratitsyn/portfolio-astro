challenge: "Bazar Bizar sells handcrafted homeware in 6 languages and 53 countries from a theme built on Dawn. The work was the storefront logic around that: sending visitors to their language and country, filters and layouts the store team can assemble without a developer, and features that would otherwise need apps."
scope:
  - "Send visitors to their language and country before the page shows."
  - "Build collection filters and product-grid layouts as theme blocks."
  - "Add a wishlist and a newsletter signup without apps."
  - "Extend the product page and the content sections of the brand."
solution:
  - "Wrote the auto-localization script: it asks Shopify for the visitor’s language and country suggestion and sends them there before the page paints. The page stays hidden for 1.2 seconds at most, a retry waits at least 30 minutes, a choice the visitor makes by hand is kept, and the script stays off in the theme editor."
  - "Built collection filters as theme blocks: the team assembles filter groups in the editor, as a list or a price range, with its own options. A price slider, an availability filter and a sale-only toggle that is kept in the URL came with it, and a clear message shows when Shopify’s price filter is missing."
  - "Built six product-grid layouts as blocks (Standard Grid, Portrait Left and Right, Double Portrait, Landscape Left and Right), each with its own product list."
  - "Built a catalogue page from one section and 7 blocks, with a button that either links or adds to the cart, a wishlist saved in the browser’s localStorage and a Mailchimp signup in the footer and the pop-up, both without an app."
  - "Extended the product page with colour swatches taken from a hex code in the variant name, a tearsheet download button, a new gallery and an added-to-cart toast, and raised collections from 12 to 48 products per page."
outcome:
  - "The live store runs in 6 languages and 53 countries, with 6 hreflang versions and x-default."
  - "Visitors reach their language and country before the page is visible, and a manual choice is never overridden."
  - "Collections show 48 products per page instead of 12."
  - "The store team builds filters and grid layouts in the editor, without code."
