What it costs to have a Shopify store built depends on the scope. That is why quotes are so far apart. Starting prices are further down, the exact price comes in the quote after the briefing.

**In short:**

- The price depends on **scope, theme, migration and special features**.
- On top come **running costs**: plan, apps, domain, payment fees, upkeep.
- Quotes are comparable only as a **fixed price after a written briefing**.
- With me: **from €500** for a store on a ready-made theme, **from €1,500** with a custom design, **from €2,000** for a migration (all prices excl. VAT).

## What the price is made of

### 1. A theme from the store or a custom theme

With a theme from the Shopify Theme Store you adapt the structure, colors and content. That is the faster and cheaper route and fits many stores. A **custom theme** to your design (for example from Figma) is built from scratch and takes more time.

### 2. Number and type of pages

A home page, a few collections and a product page are quickly built. Every further page type (home page variants, info pages, a configurator, a dealer area) adds to the scope.

### 3. Products and content

Ten products are a different job from several hundred. It also matters whether texts, images and variants are in good shape or first have to be sorted, completed or taken over from an old system.

### 4. Moving from another system

If you already have a store, for example on WooCommerce, products, customers, orders and the **redirects of old addresses** come on top, so that your Google rankings are kept. More in [Moving from WooCommerce to Shopify](/blog/woocommerce-to-shopify).

### 5. Features beyond the standard

Dealer prices and quick order for B2B, a product configurator, gift logic in the cart, a wishlist: what the theme does not bring is solved with an app or with custom code. The app usually costs a running fee, the code is paid once.

### 6. Markets, languages and currencies

One language for one market is simpler than several languages, currencies and country-specific prices and rules.

### 7. Who provides design, text and photos?

If you bring a finished design and texts, the time goes into the build. If not, a step comes before it.

## The running costs that are often forgotten

After the build these keep running:

- **The Shopify plan.** Shopify lists the current plans on its own pricing page.
- **Apps**, mostly as a monthly fee. They often slow the store too (see [Slow Shopify store? 7 causes](/blog/slow-shopify-store)).
- **Domain** and possibly your own mailbox.
- **Payment fees** per order at the payment provider.
- **Maintenance and further development**, if you do not do everything yourself.

The more the theme itself does, the fewer apps you need and the smaller the running costs.

## Fixed price or hourly rate?

**Hourly rate:** you pay for the time spent. That fits an open scope, for example ongoing support. You only know the final price at the end.

**Fixed price:** you know the price beforehand, and the risk of extra effort lies with the developer. For that the scope must be described clearly in the briefing. For projects with a clear scope I work with a **fixed price after the briefing**.

## How to tell quotes you can compare

Quotes are only comparable if they describe the same thing. Check:

1. **Which pages and features are included?** A list, not just "store build".
2. **A theme from the store or custom?**
3. **Who provides product data, texts, images?** And who transfers them into the store?
4. **Are legal texts, payments, shipping and taxes set up?**
5. **Is there a phase for corrections after launch?**
6. **Does the old store keep running until the new one is approved?**
7. **Who owns what in the end?** The store should sit on your Shopify account, not the developer's.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does the cost of a Shopify store depend on?** On scope: pages, theme, migration, special features, and who supplies the copy, images and design.

**Which costs come after launch?** The Shopify plan, paid apps, the domain, payment fees per order and, if needed, upkeep and further development.

**What does a Shopify store cost with you?** From €500 for a store on a ready-made theme, from €1,500 with a custom design and from €2,000 for a migration from another system, each excl. VAT. You get the firm number for your store after the briefing.

**Can I start with a small scope and extend later?** Yes. A store grows well in stages when the foundation is clean.

**Should the store run on my account?** Yes. You stay the owner and only give the developer access that you can withdraw at any time.

**Do I need technical knowledge?** No. You only need to know what you want to sell.
