"You need Shopify Plus for that" is only sometimes true. Much of what used to be reserved for Plus is now part of the smaller plans. The basis here is the Shopify Help Center (as of November 2026), without prices: Shopify gives you those itself, and they change.

## In short

The Help Center describes Plus as the plan for merchants selling in high volume. Switching usually pays off only when several points come together:

- changes to the **checkout steps** themselves (address, shipping, payment)
- **custom logic on the server**, written only for your store
- **B2B** with many catalogs, deposits or partial payments
- **several stores** for countries or brands
- a **team** larger than the smaller plans allow for

If none of that applies, you very likely do not need Plus.

## Checkout: the most common reason

According to the Help Center:

- **Checkout editor** for logo, colours and font: from Basic.
- **Apps for the Thank you and Order status pages**: from Basic.
- **Apps for the Information, Shipping and Payment steps**: Plus only.
- **Checkout Branding API** for styling through code: Plus only.
- **Checkout drafts**: up to 99 on Plus, 20 on the other plans.

So a notice, a field or an extra offer in the middle of the checkout needs Plus. Matching the checkout to your brand does not. Details: [Customizing the Shopify checkout](/blog/shopify-checkout-anpassen).

## Custom logic: Functions and apps

With **Shopify Functions**, custom logic runs on Shopify's servers, for example for discounts, shipping options or purchase rules. The Help Center separates two cases:

- **Public apps** from the App Store with Functions run from Basic.
- **Custom apps with Functions**, meaning logic only for your store, are listed as a Plus feature.

Before you change plans, check whether a public app already covers your special rule. More: [Having a Shopify app developed](/blog/shopify-app-entwickeln-lassen).

## B2B: works without Plus too

According to the Help Center, Shopify B2B is available on the **Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus** plans, but not on Starter or Retail. Company accounts, their own prices and catalogs are no longer a Plus topic.

Plus adds, per the Help Center:

- **unlimited catalogs**
- **direct catalog assignment to companies and locations**
- **deposits**, **partial payments** and payment requests per fulfillment
- separate checkout flows for B2B and retail customers in the same store

Count how many price lists you really need. Settle whether trade customers should pay a deposit or pay in parts. An example: [Shopify B2B: trade prices, Quick Order and rules](/blog/shopify-b2b-wholesale).

## Discounts, bundles and catalog

My other articles document these limits from the Help Center:

- **Several product discounts on the same line item** need Plus. See [Discount code or automatic discount](/blog/shopify-rabattcodes-automatische-rabatte).
- **Your own bundle offers through the APIs** are reserved for Plus. The free Shopify Bundles app is available on all plans. See [Creating Shopify bundles](/blog/shopify-bundles-erstellen).
- The **Combined Listings** app, which groups several products into one listing, is for Plus merchants. See [Product variants and options](/blog/shopify-produktvarianten-optionen).
- **Launchpad** for scheduled and automated promotions is listed as an app for Plus.
- **Shopify Audiences** is Plus only, according to the Help Center.
- **Redirects**: the limit is 100,000, on Plus 20,000,000. That only matters for very large migrations.

## Several stores and a larger team

This is where Plus differs most:

- **Unlimited staff accounts** and custom user groups for permissions.
- **9 expansion stores**, per the Help Center for internationalization, separate locations and similar purposes. Each live expansion store needs its own theme license.
- **Organization settings**: stores, users, additional security settings and billing for all stores in one place.
- **Unlimited staging stores** for testing.
- Up to **100 themes** on the account and up to **200 locations**.
- The first **20 POS Pro locations** are included.
- **Priority support** by phone and chat around the clock.

Several countries alone are not a reason for several stores. [Shopify Markets](/blog/shopify-markets-mehrere-laender) handles languages, currencies and prices per country inside one store. A second store means maintaining products, content and apps twice.

## When you do not need Plus

- **Changing how the checkout looks**: possible from Basic.
- **Selling to trade customers** with a few price lists: possible from Basic.
- **Automating**: [Shopify Flow](/blog/shopify-flow-automatisierung) is available from Basic, according to the Help Center. Only tasks from your own partner apps are reserved for Plus.
- **Selling in several countries**: Markets works without Plus.
- **Gifts, tiers or bundles**: usually possible with built-in features or a public app.
- **Slow store**: that comes from theme, apps and images, not from the plan. See [Slow Shopify store?](/blog/slow-shopify-store)

## Questions before you switch

1. **Which feature exactly is missing?** Write it down in one sentence. "More options" is not a reason.
2. **Is there a public app or a theme solution for it?** Often yes.
3. **How often do you need the feature?** One campaign a year rarely justifies a plan change.
4. **What does the build cost on top of the plan?** Plus unlocks options, it does not build them. The work on checkout or Functions comes on top.
5. **What are the term and conditions?** Settle that with Shopify directly. For the switch, the Help Center points to the Shopify Plus team.
6. **What happens if you want to go back later?** Whatever only runs on Plus would have to be replaced.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Shopify Plus for B2B?** No. According to the Help Center, Shopify B2B is available from Basic. Plus adds, among other things, unlimited catalogs, deposits and partial payments.

**Can I customize the checkout without Plus?** Yes, within limits: logo, colours, font and apps for the Thank you and Order status pages. Apps in the Information, Shipping and Payment steps are Plus only.

**Will my store get faster on Plus?** I would not count on it. Loading time depends on theme, apps and images.

**From what revenue is Plus worth it?** The Help Center gives no fixed threshold. What counts is which feature you need.

**How do I switch to Plus?** Through the Shopify Plus team. The Help Center gives no self-service steps.
