"You need Shopify Plus for that" is a sentence store owners hear a lot, and it is only sometimes true. Much of what used to be reserved for Plus is now part of the smaller plans. Other things really do need Plus. This article sorts it out using the Shopify Help Center (as of November 2026), without prices: Shopify gives you those itself, and they change.

## In short

The Help Center describes Plus as the plan for merchants selling in high volume. Switching rarely pays off for a single feature. It starts to make sense when several of these points come together:

- You want to change the **checkout steps** themselves (address, shipping, payment).
- You need **custom logic on the server**, written only for your store.
- Your **B2B business** needs many catalogs, deposits or partial payments.
- You run **several stores** for countries or brands.
- Your **team** is larger than the smaller plans allow for.

If none of that applies, you very likely do not need Plus.

## Checkout: the most common reason

This is where most misunderstandings come from. According to the Help Center:

- The **checkout editor** is available from the Basic plan. You change logo, colours and font without Plus.
- **Apps for the Thank you and Order status pages** are available from Basic.
- **Apps for the Information, Shipping and Payment steps** are available only on Plus.
- The **Checkout Branding API** for advanced styling through code is Plus only.
- Stores on Plus can keep up to **99 checkout drafts**, the other plans 20.

So if you need a notice, a field or an extra offer in the middle of the checkout, you end up at Plus. If you only want the checkout to match your brand, you do not. The details are in [Customizing the Shopify checkout](/blog/shopify-checkout-anpassen).

## Custom logic: Functions and apps

With **Shopify Functions**, custom logic runs on Shopify's servers, for example for discounts, shipping options or purchase rules. The Help Center separates two cases:

- **Public apps** from the App Store that contain Functions run from Basic.
- **Custom apps with Functions**, meaning logic written only for your store, are listed as a Plus feature.

That matters for planning: before you change plans because of one special rule, ask whether a public app already does it. More in [Having a Shopify app developed](/blog/shopify-app-entwickeln-lassen).

## B2B: works without Plus too

According to the Help Center, Shopify B2B is available on the **Basic, Grow, Advanced and Plus** plans, but not on Starter or Retail. Company accounts, their own prices and catalogs are no longer a Plus topic.

Plus adds, per the Help Center: **unlimited catalogs**, **direct catalog assignment to companies and locations**, **deposits**, **partial payments** and payment requests per fulfillment. It also allows separate checkout flows for B2B and retail customers in the same store.

For your decision: count how many price lists you really need, and settle whether trade customers should pay a deposit or pay in parts. What a shared store for trade and retail customers looks like is in [Shopify B2B: trade prices, Quick Order and rules](/blog/shopify-b2b-wholesale).

## Discounts, bundles and catalog

A few limits that my other articles document from the Help Center:

- **Several product discounts on the same line item** need Plus. See [Discount code or automatic discount](/blog/shopify-rabattcodes-automatische-rabatte).
- **Your own bundle offers through the APIs** are reserved for Plus. The free Shopify Bundles app is available on all plans. See [Creating Shopify bundles](/blog/shopify-bundles-erstellen).
- The **Combined Listings** app, which groups several products into one listing, is for Plus merchants. See [Product variants and options](/blog/shopify-produktvarianten-optionen).
- **Launchpad** for scheduled and automated promotions is listed as an app for Plus.
- **Shopify Audiences** is available on Plus only, according to the Help Center.
- For **redirects** the limit is 100,000, on Plus 20,000,000. That only matters for very large migrations.

## Several stores and a larger team

This is where Plus differs most from the rest:

- **Unlimited staff accounts** and custom user groups for assigning permissions in bulk.
- **9 expansion stores**, per the Help Center for internationalization, separate locations and similar purposes. Each live expansion store needs its own theme license.
- **Organization settings**: stores, users, additional security settings and billing for all stores in one place.
- **Unlimited staging stores** for testing.
- Up to **100 themes** on the account and up to **200 locations**.
- The first **20 POS Pro locations** are included.
- **Priority support** by phone and chat around the clock.

One caveat: several countries alone are not a reason for several stores. Shopify Markets handles languages, currencies and prices per country inside one store, see [Shopify Markets](/blog/shopify-markets-mehrere-laender). A second store also means maintaining products, content and apps twice.

## When you do not need Plus

- You want to change how the checkout **looks**: possible from Basic.
- You want to sell to **trade customers** and a few price lists are enough: possible from Basic.
- You want to **automate**: Shopify Flow is available from Basic, according to the Help Center. Only tasks from your own partner apps are reserved for Plus. See [Shopify Flow](/blog/shopify-flow-automatisierung).
- You want to sell **in several countries**: Markets works without Plus.
- You want **gifts, tiers or bundles**: usually possible with built-in features or a public app.
- The store is **slow**: that comes from theme, apps and images, not from the plan. See [Slow Shopify store?](/blog/slow-shopify-store)

## Questions before you switch

1. **Which feature exactly is missing?** Write it down in one sentence. "More options" is not a reason.
2. **Is there a public app or a theme solution for it?** Often yes.
3. **How often do you need the feature?** One campaign a year rarely justifies a plan change.
4. **What does the build cost on top of the plan?** Plus unlocks options, it does not build them. The work on checkout or Functions comes on top.
5. **What are the term and conditions?** Settle that with Shopify directly. For the switch, the Help Center points to the Shopify Plus team.
6. **What happens if you want to go back later?** Whatever only runs on Plus would have to be replaced. Ask beforehand what that would be.

At [Orska Rituals](/projects/orska-rituals), retail customers and approved professionals buy in the same store. ## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need Shopify Plus for B2B?** No. According to the Help Center, Shopify B2B is available from the Basic plan. Plus adds, among other things, unlimited catalogs, deposits and partial payments.

**Can I customize the checkout without Plus?** Yes, within limits: logo, colours, font and apps for the Thank you and Order status pages. Apps in the Information, Shipping and Payment steps are Plus only.

**Will my store get faster on Plus?** I would not count on it. Loading time depends first of all on theme, apps and images.

**From what revenue is Plus worth it?** The Help Center gives no fixed threshold. The better question is which feature you need and what it brings you.

**How do I switch to Plus?** Through the Shopify Plus team. The Help Center points to contacting Shopify and gives no self-service steps.
