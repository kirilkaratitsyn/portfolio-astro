"Shopify consulting" can mean many things: a free intro call, a written review of your store, a paid session on one specific question, or a fixed contact person over months. If you know beforehand what you need, you get more out of the conversation.

## When consulting is worth it

Mostly **before a decision** that is expensive to undo later:

- **Changing platforms.** What comes along from WooCommerce, Wix, Shopware or Magento, and what has to be rebuilt? More in [Moving from WooCommerce to Shopify](/blog/woocommerce-to-shopify).
- **Relaunch or theme change.** A new theme, or adjust the current one? See [Hiring someone for a Shopify relaunch](/blog/shopify-relaunch-theme-wechsel).
- **New markets or trade customers.** Several countries, languages and currencies, or trade prices, change how the store is built. See [Shopify Markets](/blog/shopify-markets-mehrere-laender) and [Shopify B2B](/blog/shopify-b2b-wholesale).
- **App or customization.** Often an app is enough, sometimes a change in the theme is cleaner, rarely you need a custom app. See [Having a Shopify app built](/blog/shopify-app-entwickeln-lassen).
- **Quotes you cannot compare.** Each one describes something different. A second opinion on the scope often saves more than it costs.
- **A problem that has been there for weeks.** The store is slow or a feature does not work, and nobody feels responsible.

## When you do not need consulting

- **You cannot find a setting or do not understand a feature:** use the Shopify Help Center, or Shopify Support for payments, plan or domain.
- **An app misbehaves:** write to the app developer first.

Who handles what is covered in [Shopify help: when the Help Center is enough](/blog/shopify-help). Consulting makes sense once it is about **your** store: your theme, your apps, your plans.

## Four kinds of consulting

**1. Intro call.** Usually short and free. It clarifies what this is about, whether the person is a fit and what comes next. You do not get a finished concept: without looking into the store, nobody can estimate the work.

**2. Shop-Check or review.** Someone writes down what stands out in your store. Load time, embedded apps and page structure are visible from the outside. Settings and theme code need admin access.

**3. Paid consulting on a specific question.** For example: which data comes along in a migration? How do we handle trade prices? The result should be in writing, so another developer can work with it too.

**4. Ongoing support.** A fixed contact person who knows the store. Worth it for stores that change things all the time. More in [Shopify maintenance and support](/blog/shopify-wartung-betreuung).

## Who does the consulting

Agencies and freelancers advise on your store. Shopify Support only handles the platform. According to the Shopify Help Center, the **Shopify Partner Directory** lists established agency partners from a certain tier in the Partner Program. Someone who is not listed is not necessarily worse. The comparison is in [Shopify freelancer or agency](/blog/shopify-freelancer-oder-agentur).

## How to prepare for the conversation

Write down beforehand:

1. **Your store address.**
2. **Your goal in one sentence**, for example "trade customers should be able to reorder without calling".
3. **What you have already tried**, and with what result.
4. **Your apps**, and which of them you really need.
5. **A deadline**, such as a trade fair, a season or the cancellation date of the old system.
6. **The scale:** a small fix or a bigger rebuild.
7. **Who decides.** Ideally that person joins the call.

## What you should have in hand at the end

- a **written summary**,
- **next steps** in a clear order,
- what **you can do yourself** and what you need help with,
- what the consulting **could not** settle, such as tax or legal questions,
- for a quote: a described scope that you can compare.

## Questions you should ask

- **Can you show examples of similar stores?**
- **Who will work on my store**, you or someone else?
- **How do you get access?** The usual way is a collaborator account with limited permissions. Your owner login stays with you. Details in [Switching your Shopify developer](/blog/shopify-entwickler-wechseln).
- **Do you earn anything from apps or themes you recommend?** A commission is not forbidden, but you should know about it.
- **What happens if things go differently than planned?**

## Warning signs

- **Advice without having seen the store.**
- **A complete rebuild right away**, with no reason why changes are not enough.
- **A request for your owner password.**
- **Guarantees on revenue or Google rankings.**
- **No written result.**
