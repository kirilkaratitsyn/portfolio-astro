## 2. Hosting

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., USA. Each time a page is requested, Vercel automatically processes data your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, referrer URL, browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the website and protect it against abuse.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in a secure, working website. Vercel processes the data on my behalf. Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which is the basis for the transfer to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR). Vercel keeps these logs for this project for at most one day. Details: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

## 3. Contact by email

If you email me, I process what you send (name, email address, message) to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry aims at a contract, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. I delete the data once it is no longer needed, unless statutory retention periods apply.

## 4. Booking a call via Cal.com

You can book an introductory call through Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc., USA). The website loads the Cal.com booking window only when you click a booking button; at that point your IP address and device information are sent to Cal.com. When you book, Cal.com processes what you enter (name, email address, notes, chosen time).

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps prior to a contract). Cal.com is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which is the basis for the transfer to the USA (Art. 45 GDPR). In the booking window Cal.com sets cookies for the session and for abuse protection (Cloudflare) and keeps entries in local storage, for example for the time format and a held booking. These cookies and entries belong to Cal.com, not to this website. Details: https://cal.com/privacy

## 8. Emails to Shopify stores

I offer Shopify development to online stores. To do this, a partner emails Shopify stores in the USA, the United Kingdom and Australia on my behalf.

For this I process the store's name, the business email address the store has published itself and the page where it appears, as well as the date of the email and your reply if you answer. The source of this data is the store's public website.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, my legitimate interest in offering my services to businesses (direct marketing, Recital 47 GDPR).

I keep the data until you object. After that I keep only your email address with a "do not contact" note, so that you get no further emails.

The contact list is kept in Notion (Notion Labs, Inc., USA) and the emails are sent through Microsoft 365. Both process the data on my behalf. Where data goes to the USA, the transfer relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, under which Notion Labs, Inc. and Microsoft Corporation are certified (Art. 45 GDPR).

You can object to this processing at any time (Art. 21(2) GDPR), for example with a short reply to the email or by writing to kiril@karatitsyn.com. I will then not contact you again. Your other rights are in the next section.
